About the Data

This project explores global fuel price patterns and examines their relationship with taxation, income levels and government subsidy policies. The dataset provides insights into fuel price variations across countries and regions over time.

Dataset Overview

The dataset contains weekly observations covering the period from January 2020 to April 2026.

Dataset Variables

Country, Date, Income Level, Region, Subsidy Level, Brent Crude Price, Petrol Price, Diesel Price, LPG Price and Tax Percentage.

Project Objective

To analyze global fuel price trends, compare regional differences, understand taxation patterns and explore the role of government subsidies and income levels.
